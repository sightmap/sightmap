## Contacting maintainers

- **Security reports** — subtext@fullstory.com (see [`SECURITY.md`](SECURITY.md))

## Becoming a maintainer

See [`GOVERNANCE.md#maintainer`](https://github.com/sightmap/.github/blob/main/GOVERNANCE.md#maintainer). The short version: sustained high-quality contributions + nomination + consensus. There is no fixed threshold, and we prefer deliberate additions over frequent ones.

If you are interested in contributing at that level, the best path is to start by participating in SEP discussions, reviewing PRs (anyone can leave review comments), and gradually taking on larger changes.
